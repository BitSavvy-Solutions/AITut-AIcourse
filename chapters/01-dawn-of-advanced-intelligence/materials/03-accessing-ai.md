# How to Access AI Safely and Fairly Cheaply

There are two common ways to use AI systems.

## AI Interfaces

Most people access AI through websites and applications.

Examples:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

These tools provide easy access without any programming.

---

## AI APIs

Developers can connect directly to AI models through APIs.

An API allows software to send requests to an AI model and receive responses automatically.

This makes it possible to build:

- Chatbots
- AI assistants
- Automation tools
- Business applications

---

## Using AI Responsibly

When using AI:

- Verify important information
- Avoid sharing sensitive personal data
- Respect copyright and privacy
- Use AI as an assistant rather than a replacement for critical thinking

---

## Key Takeaway

AI can be accessed through user-friendly applications or through APIs for software development. Understanding both approaches is an important foundation for future AI work.
