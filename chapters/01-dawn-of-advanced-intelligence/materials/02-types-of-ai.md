# The Different Kinds of AI

AI systems come in many forms.

## Text AI

Text AI works with written language.

Examples:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini

These systems can answer questions, summarize information, and help with writing.

---

## Image AI

Image AI creates or edits images from text instructions.

Examples:

- Midjourney
- Stable Diffusion
- DALL·E

---

## Audio AI

Audio AI can generate speech, transcribe recordings, or create music.

Examples:

- ElevenLabs
- Whisper
- Suno

---

## Video AI

Video AI can create videos from prompts or edit existing footage.

Examples:

- Runway
- Kling
- Veo

---

## Key Takeaway

Modern AI is no longer limited to text. It can work with images, audio, video, and many combinations of these formats.
