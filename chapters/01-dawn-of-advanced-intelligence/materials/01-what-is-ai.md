# What Exactly is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

- Understanding language
- Generating text
- Creating images
- Recognizing speech
- Making predictions
- Solving problems

AI does not think like a human being. Instead, it learns patterns from large amounts of data and uses those patterns to generate responses or make decisions.

## Why AI Matters

AI is becoming one of the most important technologies of our time.

It is being used in:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Software Development
- Marketing
- Research
- Customer Support

Learning how AI works will help you become more productive and prepare for the future workplace.

## Key Takeaway

AI is a tool that can assist humans in solving problems, generating ideas, and automating repetitive work.
